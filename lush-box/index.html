The Lush Box

Finding the stronger market for a corporate gifting business.

Co-founderBusiness Innovation Lead2021 to 2025

The Lush Box began with a simple idea: create thoughtful gift boxes using carefully selected products, including locally produced items where possible. Early orders proved that customers valued the concept, but every box required sourcing, packaging, pricing and delivery. We needed to understand which customer segment could support a stronger business.

The commercial question

Individual and corporate customers wanted different things. Individual orders were highly personal, while corporate customers needed larger quantities, consistent presentation, clear quotations and dependable delivery. I wanted to understand where value was being created and where margin was being lost once the operational effort behind each order was considered.

How I worked

I tracked customer demand, incoming payments, margins and the work involved in sourcing, packaging and fulfilment. I compared individual and corporate orders, worked directly with customers to turn loosely defined needs into practical proposals, and coordinated pricing, suppliers, inventory and delivery.

The first customer payments arrived in July 2021. During the following months, we tested products and fulfilled individual orders generally worth a few hundred Danish kroner each. By November, the order pattern had changed. Larger corporate orders required a more structured operating model and offered a clearer route to growth.

The decision

The evidence helped us concentrate on corporate gifting. We moved retail orders to an e-commerce model and developed a separate process for corporate customers instead of forcing both segments through the same system. This made it easier to tailor proposals, price the work and plan fulfilment according to the size and complexity of each order.

Across the visible 2021 records, the business received approximately 140,000 DKK in customer payments. The largest recorded customer payment was above 100,000 DKK. These figures are presented without naming the customers involved.

Building for growth

The shift required more than attracting larger orders. We strengthened supplier relationships, developed clearer quotations and reporting, and created more repeatable processes for purchasing, assembly and international delivery. We partnered with Danish entrepreneurs, included locally produced products where possible and reduced unnecessary single-use materials.

In our first year, we delivered more than 500 curated gift boxes. Over the life of the business, we served more than ten corporate clients and shipped gifts to 33 countries.

What this experience developed

Building, running and reshaping The Lush Box taught me to connect customer behaviour, margin data and operational reality. It also taught me that growth depends on choosing where to focus, designing different ways to serve different customers and building the systems that allow a promising idea to become a dependable business.
