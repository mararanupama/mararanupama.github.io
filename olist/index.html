E-commerce Sales and Delivery Performance

Using SQL and Power BI to compare sales, seller performance and delivery reliability across Brazil.

Explore the interactive report

Use the four report pages and their filters to explore market geography, sales and assortment, the seller network, and delivery experience. Monetary values are in Brazilian reais (R$).

Open the report in a separate tab for a larger view, especially on a phone.

KPI definitions: what the numbers mean

KPIs are key performance indicators. These definitions follow the report's measures and apply within the selected filters. All monetary amounts are in Brazilian reais (R$).

Sales

Delivered revenue
Total product value from delivered orders, excluding freight charges.
Delivered orders / product orders
Distinct delivered orders. On product and seller views, only orders containing the selected items count. An order can appear in more than one category.
Average order value
Delivered revenue divided by the number of delivered orders.
Freight charged / freight %
Freight charged to customers for delivered items. Freight % is this amount divided by delivered product revenue, multiplied by 100. It measures charges, not delivery costs.
Average freight per order
Freight charged for selected delivered items divided by distinct delivered orders containing those items.

Customers and sellers

Delivered customers
Unique customers with at least one delivered order.
States served
Customer states with at least one delivered order.
Active sellers
Unique sellers with at least one delivered sale.
Revenue per seller
Delivered product revenue divided by active sellers.

Delivery and reviews

Average delivery days
Mean recorded delivery duration for delivered orders. Missing durations are excluded.
Observed late-delivery rate
Late orders divided by delivered orders with both actual and promised delivery dates, multiplied by 100. Lateness is compared by calendar date.
On-time rate
The percentage of those observed deliveries that arrived on or before the promised date. Equal to 100% minus the observed late-delivery rate.
Average review score
Mean of each order's average review score. Orders without a review are excluded.
Low review share
Reviewed orders with an average score of 2 or below, divided by all reviewed orders, multiplied by 100.

The business question

A marketplace can grow its sales while customers in some regions still face slow deliveries or high freight charges. I used SQL and Power BI to bring commercial performance and customer experience into a single report, allowing readers to compare where demand comes from, which sellers contribute to it and how delivery performance varies.

Building a reliable analytical model

The model imports MySQL analytical views for orders, order items and customer summaries, alongside customer, product and seller dimensions. Order-level and item-level facts are kept separate so that a multi-item order does not automatically become several orders in the analysis.

Separate customer and seller geography dimensions distinguish where demand originates from where sellers are based. DAX measures calculate delivered revenue, average order value, active sellers and delivery performance within the selected context.

SQL and geographic data quality

The accompanying geography SQL script uses common table expressions and window functions to deduplicate postcode coordinates, calculate median locations and select a single state for each postcode. It also includes checks comparing customer counts before and after the geography join, helping detect joins that could multiply records.

The project notes identify the geography in the report as a fixed snapshot prepared from local CSV files. The SQL script provides its database-side equivalent; execution of that script is not established by the supplied files.

Four views of marketplace performance

  • Market Geography: compare customer revenue, regional demand and service performance across Brazil.
  • Sales & Assortment: explore monthly delivered merchandise revenue, leading categories and freight charged.
  • Seller Network: examine seller concentration, leading sellers and regional commercial contribution.
  • Delivery Experience: compare late deliveries, delivery times and review outcomes.

Making the measures meaningful

Delivered revenue includes merchandise prices for delivered orders and excludes freight. Freight charged is analysed separately; it does not represent logistics cost or profit margin. The observed late-delivery rate uses delivered orders with both actual and estimated delivery dates, so missing dates are not treated as evidence of on-time delivery.

Category order counts are distinct within the selected category. Because a basket can contain several categories, adding those counts together would overstate the number of orders. Seller and category revenue rankings recalculate within the current selections.

Decisions the report supports

The report gives a marketplace team a way to investigate whether commercially important regions also experience delivery problems, how dependent sales are on particular sellers, and where freight charges deserve closer review. These comparisons can guide follow-up analysis and operational prioritisation.

Limits of the analysis

This is a historical public dataset. Differences between regions, sellers or review groups describe associations and do not establish causes. Revenue is not profit, and customer freight charges are not the marketplace's delivery costs.
