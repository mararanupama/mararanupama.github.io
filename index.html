My journey

I sit between the data and the people making the call.

Over the years I have worked across banking, entrepreneurship and technology. The common thread is translating complexity into something a team can act on with confidence.

I care about what analysis helps someone decide. Good analysis makes its assumptions visible, connects evidence with commercial reality and remains useful when the decision is difficult.

What I bring

Business analysis

Across treasury, corporate credit and branch leadership, I learned to connect commercial opportunity with risk and operational reality. I turned financial and operational evidence into credit decisions, performance reporting and practical action, while helping grow a new branch from zero to approximately ₹200 million, or USD 2.4 million, in business within its first year.

Commercial judgment

Building, running and reshaping my own company taught me to see where a business created value and where margin was being lost. I used customer behaviour, margin data and operational effort to redesign how we served individual and corporate customers, then strengthened the supplier relationships, fulfilment processes and reporting needed to support growth.

Responsible AI implementation

At an early-stage technology startup, testing showed that AI-generated humour lacked the context needed to feel natural. I helped redirect the work towards a conversational voice experience with clearer value for users.
